JARVIS CHESS - INSTALLATION GUIDE
==================================

What you're installing:
A desktop chess game where you play against the computer.

System Requirements:
- Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- 500 MB free disk space
- Internet connection (optional, for chess.com import)

Installation Steps:
===================

1. Download
   - Go to: https://github.com/AGENT17-tech/JARVIS-Chess/releases
   - Click "v2.0.0" release
   - Download: JARVIS-Chess-Setup.exe

2. Install
   - Find JARVIS-Chess-Setup.exe in your Downloads folder
   - Double-click it
   - Windows will ask: "Do you want to run this file?" → Click "Yes"
   - An installer window appears
   - Click the "Install" button
   - Wait 30 seconds for installation to complete

3. Launch
   - Click "Play" in the installer window
   - OR: Look for "JARVIS Chess" in your Start Menu
   - Click to launch

4. Play
   - Click "New Game"
   - Choose difficulty (1-20)
   - Click or drag pieces to move
   - JARVIS plays automatically

That's it! Enjoy.

Troubleshooting:
================

Q: I get a "Windows protected your PC" message
A: Click "More info" → "Run anyway"
   (This is normal for new apps)

Q: The app won't start after installation
A: 1. Restart your computer
   2. Try running again

Q: Can I uninstall it?
A: Yes - Settings → Apps → Apps & features → JARVIS Chess → Uninstall
